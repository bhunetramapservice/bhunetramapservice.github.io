What we collect and why

  • Enquiry and review forms: the name, mobile, email and village details you type, only to answer your enquiry, prepare a quotation and deliver your order. The form is delivered to our email through FormSubmit.
  • WhatsApp / email: what you send us, for the same purpose.
  • Visitor counter: GoatCounter counts page views without cookies and without storing your IP address.
  • Order tracker and invoice check: only a one-way code of the invoice number and amount is published - no names, no amounts.

We do not sell or share your details with anyone, and we keep them only as long as needed for your order and our accounts. Write to us to see, correct or delete your details (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023).

Maps and land records

Our maps are digitized from public land-record maps published by the states and georeferenced on satellite imagery. They are for planning and reference: they are not a legal survey, a title document or a certified copy of any government record. Always check with the revenue department (patwari / tehsil / survey office) before any legal use. Owner names are not published on this website.

Not a government website

BHU-NETRA Map Service is a private service. It is not connected with NIC, Bhunaksha or any state government. Names of government portals are used only to describe where the official maps are; their links lead to the official websites.
