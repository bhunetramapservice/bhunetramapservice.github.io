How to use Bhunaksha

  1. Open your state's portal from the table.
  2. Choose district, tehsil (taluk / circle / mandal) and village.
  3. Click a plot to see its khasra / survey / dag number and area; some states also show the owner record.
  4. Need the map as a file for a project? Send us the village names for a quotation.

Questions

What is Bhunaksha?

Bhunaksha is the cadastral-map software developed by NIC (National Informatics Centre) that many states use to show their village maps online. You choose the district, tehsil and village and see every plot with its number.

Bhunaksha is not working - what can I do?

State Bhunaksha portals are often busy, slow or under maintenance, and some need a different browser. Try again later or at night, or send us the village name - we deliver the village map as KML, DWG, SHP, Excel or PDF.

Can I download a Bhunaksha map as KML?

The portals show a picture of the map, not a GIS file. We turn the same village map into a georeferenced KML / KMZ that opens in Google Earth, plus DWG / DXF for AutoCAD and SHP for QGIS / ArcGIS.

How do I measure an area from Bhunaksha?

On the portal you can read the plot area from the plot details. On our KML file, Google Earth's ruler (polygon) measures any area in hectares or acres directly on the georeferenced map.

Is BHU-NETRA Map Service a government site?

No. BHU-NETRA Map Service is a private map service. The official records stay on the state portals linked below.
