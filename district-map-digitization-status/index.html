Questions

What does 'Available' mean?

We can deliver the digitized, georeferenced map of villages in that district now, from the state's public land-record map. Every order is checked village by village; a few villages may be missing on the state portal itself - we tell you in the quotation.

Is this the government's DILRMP progress?

No. It is our own delivery status. The government's progress reports are on the DILRMP and state land-record websites.

My district is not listed?

Write to us - new states and districts are added regularly.
