DOCUMENT CHECK

Is your invoice genuine?

Every BHU-NETRA invoice and quotation is digitally signed. Scan its QR code, paste the QR text, or enter the document number and total amount – the check runs right here in your browser.

Point the camera at the QR code on the invoice (next to "SCAN • VERIFY"). Nothing is uploaded – the picture stays on your device.

Use the TOTAL AMOUNT printed on the document (before advance), e.g. 1,14,000.00.

How it works: the QR holds the document details and a digital signature (Ed25519) that only BHU-NETRA Map Service can make. If anyone changes a number, the signature no longer matches. The number + amount check looks the document up in our published list, which holds only scrambled fingerprints – no client names or amounts.

Still unsure? Write to bhunetramapservice@gmail.com with a photo of the document.
