Land record & revenue websites of every state: Bhulekh, Jamabandi, Bhunaksha

By BHU-NETRA Map Service · updated 16 Nov 2026

Every state runs its own revenue department website for land records: Apna Khata and Bhunaksha in Rajasthan, UP Bhulekh in Uttar Pradesh, Mahabhulekh in Maharashtra, Bihar Bhumi, Jamabandi in Haryana and Punjab, Dharani in Telangana and so on. The table lists the official site for each state, the record it gives (jamabandi, khatauni, 7/12, RTC, patta) and the georeferenced khasra map we can prepare for the same state.

These are government sites, not run by us. Portal addresses can change, so confirm on the state revenue department page.

What the government portals do not give you

  • The portal shows one village sheet or one khasra at a time. A project needs the whole village, or 50 villages, in one georeferenced file.
  • Portal maps are not georeferenced for download. Solar, wind, transmission and highway teams need KML, DWG or SHP to overlay on satellite imagery, a layout or a route.
  • Land category (private, allotable government land, non-allotable) is not colour-coded on the portal.

That is the gap BHU-NETRA fills: village maps of any khasra, survey number or gat number as georeferenced KML, DWG, SHP and Excel, for 20 states. Read the land-record terms of each state, or pick your state.
