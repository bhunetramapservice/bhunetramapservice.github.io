How to get the cadastral (revenue) map of any village in India

BHU-NETRA Map Service · 20-10-2026

A cadastral map - also called the revenue map, village map, khasra map or survey map - shows how a village is divided into plots, each with its own number. It is the base for every land project: buying, leasing, solar and wind farms, roads, transmission lines and pipelines.

Where the map comes from

Each state keeps its village maps with the revenue / survey department, and most now publish them on a public portal (Bhunaksha, Bhulekh, Bhoomi, TNGIS, Mahabhunakasha, Ente Bhoomi and others). The written record of rights (jamabandi, khatauni, 7/12, RTC, patta, adangal) is kept separately.

What you need to find a village

  1. State and district
  2. Tehsil / taluk / mandal / circle (the unit above the village)
  3. Village name - and, if you have it, the census or LGD code
  4. Optional: the plot numbers you are interested in

Not sure of the spelling? Use the village search on our website: it lists about 4.5 lakh census villages of the states we cover.

Turning the map into project files

  • KML / KMZ - to see the plots on Google Earth and share with the land team
  • DWG / DXF - for engineering layouts in AutoCAD, in real UTM coordinates
  • SHP - for GIS analysis in QGIS / ArcGIS, with the plot attributes
  • Excel - plot numbers and areas for the land budget

One village or a whole district

Single-village orders are common - for a plot purchase or a quick site check. For projects, all villages around the site are delivered together, joined edge to edge in one combined layer with village boundaries.
