Bhunaksha not working? What to do, and how to get the village map anyway

BHU-NETRA Map Service · 20-10-2026

Bhunaksha portals carry a lot of load: whole districts look up land at the same time, and the servers are often slow or down for maintenance. Here is what usually helps - and what to do when it still does not open.

Common reasons

  • Server busy at office hours - the page loads blank or the map tiles stay grey
  • Maintenance or a changed address of the state portal
  • Old security settings on the portal - some browsers refuse the connection
  • Too many requests from one network - the portal asks for a captcha or stops answering

What to try

  1. Open the portal again after some time, or early morning / late night
  2. Try another browser, or switch off a VPN or ad-blocker
  3. Check the address on your state page - the link list of every state is on our Bhunaksha page
  4. Zoom in slowly - the map draws only when a village is selected

Need the map for work? Get it as a file

A Bhunaksha screen is a picture. For Google Earth, AutoCAD or GIS you need the map as a georeferenced file anyway. Send us the state, district, tehsil and village names: we deliver the village map with every plot as a polygon and its number, in KML, DWG / DXF, SHP and Excel, and a PDF map sheet on request - one village or a whole district.

BHU-NETRA Map Service is a private service and is not connected with NIC or any state government; the official records stay with the state portal.
