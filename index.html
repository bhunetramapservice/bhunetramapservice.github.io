SURVEY SERVICES

All types of survey work

From a single plot to a whole project corridor - field survey teams with DGPS, Total Station and drones, and every result delivered in the same formats as our maps (KML, DWG, SHP, PDF).

⌖

DGPS survey

Centimetre-level control points and boundary pillars with differential GPS - for legal-grade positions and to tie maps to the ground.

△

Total Station survey

Detailed field measurement of plots, roads, structures and features, delivered as CAD drawings in real coordinates.

≈

Topographic & contour survey

Ground levels, contours and spot heights for solar layouts, grading, drainage and earthwork (cut & fill) design.

✈

Drone (UAV) survey

High-resolution orthophotos, digital surface / terrain models and 3D views of your site, flown and processed end to end.

▢

Land demarcation & boundary survey

Khasra and property boundaries marked on the ground from the land records - with the georeferenced map as the base.

⟶

Route survey

Corridors for transmission lines, roads, pipelines and canals: alignment, crossings and the khasras affected along the route.

☀

Solar & wind site survey

Site feasibility: land category, slope, obstacles, access and available area - combined with our khasra and govt-land maps.

☰

Layout & plotting plans

Plot, colony and project layout plans prepared on the surveyed base, ready for approval and sale.

✎

FMB preparation

Field Measurement Book sketches for every survey number - measured on the ground, drawn to scale with chains, offsets and neighbours, georeferenced.

✱

WTG micro-siting survey

Turbine points checked against the MNRE setbacks (hub height + half rotor + 5 m from roads, buildings and lines; 500 m from villages), staked with DGPS, with the khasras under each WTG.

☷

Land acquisition schedule

Khasra-wise affected area, owner details from the records and the land schedule in Excel - for leases, purchase and compensation.

▦

Sub-division & partition survey

Splitting a khasra on the ground and on the map - new boundaries, areas and a sketch ready for the revenue records.

FAQ
Questions clients ask

    

      
What exactly do I receive?
Georeferenced village maps in which every khasra is a closed polygon carrying its khasra number, plus the village outer boundary, an Excel khasra list with areas, and - if you ask for it - a colour-coded land category map and one combined layer for all your villages.

      
Which file formats do you deliver?
KML / KMZ (Google Earth, Google Maps), DWG / DXF (AutoCAD and any CAD), SHP shapefile with a ready QGIS style (QGIS, ArcGIS), Excel (.xlsx) and PDF / PNG print maps. Tell us your software and we deliver in that format.

      
Do you also do field surveys?
Yes - all types of survey work: DGPS, Total Station, topographic and contour surveys, drone (UAV) mapping, land demarcation and boundary surveys, route surveys for transmission lines and roads, solar and wind site surveys, and layout plans.

      
Is there a minimum order?
No. A single village map is welcome, and so is a whole tehsil or district.

      
Which states do you cover?
Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Odisha, Chhattisgarh, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Haryana, Delhi, Goa, Tripura, Kerala, Punjab, Jharkhand, Assam, Telangana and Himachal Pradesh. Write to us for other states - new states are added regularly.

      
How accurate is the georeferencing?
Every village is placed in real-world coordinates (WGS 84 / UTM) and aligned to satellite imagery, so the khasra lines sit on the visible field edges. The maps are digitized from public land-record maps: they are excellent for planning, land aggregation and layout design, but they are not a legal survey - for legal accuracy we offer DGPS / Total Station surveys.

      
Can government and private land be shown separately?
Yes. Where the state's land records provide it, khasras are colour-coded: yellow for private / khatedari land, red for non-allotable government land and green for allotable government land (forest and other classes on request).

      
How long does delivery take?
The delivery time is written in every quotation and depends on the number of villages or the survey area. Files are shared on Google Drive (or a pen drive for very large orders), folder-wise: District > Tehsil > Village.

      
How do I pay?
50% advance with the order and the balance before the final files are handed over, by UPI or bank transfer. Every invoice is digitally signed.

      
How can I check that an invoice is genuine?
Open bhunetramapservice.github.io/verify and scan the QR code on the invoice, or enter the invoice number and total amount.

      
Are the digitized maps legal documents?
No. They are digitized from public land-record maps for planning and reference use. For any legal purpose, check the official record with the Patwari / Tehsil office, or order a DGPS demarcation survey.
